    BEFOREYOU RESERVE

    Can I choose what is in a blind box?

    No. Each box holds two random small pieces, or the full-cover piece on its own. That is the point.

    Can I put small pieces on top of the full-cover piece?

    No. Hook does not grip hook. A full-cover piece takes the whole canvas.

    What does the NFC chip do?

    Tap a rare piece with your phone. A sealed chip shows "Genuine Statch — sealed". Once you register it, it shows the piece and its serial number.

    When does it ship?

    After production samples are approved. We will email the date before we charge you, and tell you straight away if anything slips.

    Will future badges fit this tee?

    Yes. Every series fits the same 100 × 80 mm canvas.
